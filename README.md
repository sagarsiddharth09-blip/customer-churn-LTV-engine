# Customer Churn Prediction & LTV Engine

## Project Overview

A predictive analytics system designed to identify customers at high risk of churn and estimate Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

## Objective

- Identify customers at high risk of churn
- Analyze factors influencing customer churn
- Predict Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Support data-driven customer retention strategies
